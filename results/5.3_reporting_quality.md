### Effects of delayed and missing readings
- Delayed readings make an early report temporarily low. Only readings from the last about 3 hours of the day
  can still be in transit at midnight (maximum delay 180 minutes), so the midnight total was only 0.10 %
  (152 kWh) below the final one, and it was complete by 02:45.
- Missing readings never arrive, so they lower totals permanently. On 21 September 1.03 % of readings
  were missing; if they resembled observed readings, about 1,560 kWh (about 1.04 %) is absent from even the final
  total, about 10 times the effect of delays.
- Practice: publish daily totals as provisional at midnight and final after about 3 hours; always report
  completeness alongside totals, and never treat missing readings as zero consumption.
