## 6.2 Freshness and delayed arrivals

Program: src/freshness_test.py (full log: results/6_2_freshness_test.txt).
Test hypertable freshness_test (event_time TIMESTAMPTZ, energy_kwh DOUBLE PRECISION), hourly continuous
aggregate freshness_hourly (CAT hour bucket, sum(energy_kwh), count(*)), created WITH NO DATA,
materialized_only = true, no refresh policy.

Test time 2026-10-08 19:53:26 CAT. H = 2026-10-08 18:00-19:00 CAT (last completed hour).
Older hour = H - 48 h = 2026-10-06 18:00-19:00 CAT.
Inserts: 1 kWh at 2026-10-06 18:10, 0.5 kWh at 2026-10-06 18:25, 2 kWh at 2026-10-08 18:10 (CAT).

| Stage | Older-hour aggregate | Hour H aggregate | Raw table (older / H) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Older hour refreshed, real-time off | 1.0 kWh, 1 row | no bucket returned | 1.0 / - |
| 2. Both new inserts, no refresh, real-time off | 1.0 kWh, 1 row | no bucket returned | 1.5 kWh 2 rows / 2.0 kWh 1 row |
| 3. Real-time on, no refresh | 1.0 kWh, 1 row | 2.0 kWh, 1 row | 1.5 / 2.0 |
| 4. Both hours refreshed (real-time off again) | 1.5 kWh, 2 rows | 2.0 kWh, 1 row | 1.5 kWh 2 rows / 2.0 kWh 1 row |

Check: older hour 1.5 kWh / 2 rows and hour H 2 kWh / 1 row after refresh: PASS.

Explanation:
- Inserts never update stored buckets; with real-time off the aggregate shows only what was last refreshed.
- Real-time aggregation adds raw data only after the watermark (end of the newest stored bucket,
  2026-10-06 19:00). Hour H was after it and appeared (2 kWh); the late 0.5 kWh fell in an already
  materialised hour before the watermark, so the stale 1 kWh was still returned.
- Only a refresh covering the older hour made it correct. An absent bucket means "not yet computed",
  not zero consumption.
- 6.1 policy at the test time: window 2026-10-05 19:53:26 to 2026-10-08 18:53:26 CAT. The older hour was
  fully inside and could be refreshed; hour H ended after the window end and could not be refreshed until
  20:00 CAT. Delayed readings (up to 3 h late) therefore reach the aggregates through the 3-day lookback.
