## Observations

- Weekday profile peaks around 13:00 (about 7,650 kW on average); weekend profile is lower and
  peaks later, around 18:45 (about 6,000 kW), reflecting reduced commercial/industrial load.
- The three highest intervals are all on the last Monday, the combination of the weekday midday
  peak and the upward weekly trend.
- Capacity planning should target weekday middays, with headroom for continued growth.
