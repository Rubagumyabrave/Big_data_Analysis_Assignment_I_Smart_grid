## 3.1 Three chunk configurations

Built with sql/06_chunk_configs.sql, verified with sql/07_chunk_checks.sql (2026-10-08 CAT).
All three are hypertables partitioned by event_time with the same eight columns, copied from the
cleaned energy_readings.

| Table | Chunk interval | Chunks | First chunk start | Last chunk end |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| energy_readings_3h | 3 hours | 225 | 2026-08-31 23:00 CAT | 2026-09-29 02:00 CAT |
| energy_readings | 1 day | 29 | 2026-08-31 02:00 CAT | 2026-09-29 02:00 CAT |
| energy_readings_week | 7 days | 5 | 2026-08-27 02:00 CAT | 2026-10-01 02:00 CAT |

Chunk boundaries are aligned to UTC (3-hour and daily boundaries at UTC midnight = 02:00 CAT; weekly
boundaries on Thursdays, counted from 1970-01-01). The 28 CAT days therefore span 224+1, 28+1 and 5 chunks.

Indexes (identical method and sort order on all three tables):
- btree (event_time DESC)
- btree (meter_id, event_time DESC)
