### Observations

- All six measured counts match the generator's reference values exactly.
- Missing and completeness use planned readings as the denominator; duplicates and delays use
  observed unique pairs, because only readings that arrived can be duplicated or delayed.
- The diagnostic run took about 2 minutes. Grouping 10.7 M rows (44 s) and the anti-join against
  the 10.75 M-row grid (56 s) were the slowest steps, because they exceed work_mem (15 MB) and
  spill to disk.
