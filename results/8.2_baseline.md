# 8.2 Previous-day baseline

Script: src\forecast_8_2_baseline.py (run 2026-10-09 09:30:59 CAT)
Method: at 00:00 CAT on each test day (22-28 Sep), each of the 24 hours is forecast as the same
hour of the previous day (lag_24h), for all 10 regions. Error = predicted - actual (kWh).

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|

| Every source hour precedes its origin | True |

Sat 26 135.695 | Sun 27 3.840 | Mon 28 139.461

Observation: on days that follow the same day type the baseline misses by ~5 kWh (<1 % of a
~590 kWh hour). Almost all its error comes from the two day-type switches: Saturday forecast from
Friday (weekday level) and Monday forecast from Sunday (weekend level), consistent with the 21.3 %
weekend drop found in 5.1.
