### Differences that remain after normalisation, and implications
- Customer type: a large, real difference remains. An industrial meter uses 17.5x and a commercial
  meter 2.6x the energy of a residential meter; 10 % of meters (industrial) use 57.6 % of all energy.
- Region: every region has 400 meters, yet energy per meter differs by up to 1.55x. Almost all of this is
  explained by the customer mix (r = 0.97 with the industrial share); within the same customer type,
  regions differ by only 6-13 %, consistent with individual meters' different base loads rather than
  a regional effect.
- Implications: network capacity and investment should follow customer mix (Region 8 carries about 55 %
  more energy per meter than Region 4); industrial customers are the priority for demand-response and peak
  tariffs; forecasts and anomaly thresholds should be set per customer type, not one value for all meters.
- Completeness is not a factor: every region has about 1.064 million readings (see 5.3).
